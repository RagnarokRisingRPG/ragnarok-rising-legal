# Ragnarok Rising: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Ragnarok Rising: The War of the Nine Realms ("the game") is made by 99Gaming Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and what we do with it. Questions or requests: ragnarokrisingrpg@gmail.com

## Who the game is for
The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

## What we collect
- **Device identifier.** A device ID provided by your phone's operating system (or a random ID the game creates) so the server can tell your device apart from others and keep your account linked to it.
- **Account details you choose.** Your player name, your character's class and appearance (gender, hair, eye color, expression), and whether your account is a Drengr or a Valhalla account.
- **Game data.** Gacha pity counters and a record of whether a purchase has been made. Most progress (gear, currencies, settings) is saved on your device.
- **Technical data.** Your IP address and request details appear in our server logs when the game connects. The game also uses Unity's engine diagnostics, which can send crash and performance reports (device model, operating system version, error details).
- **Purchases.** If in-app purchases are available, the Apple App Store or Google Play handles payment. We never see your card number.

We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

## How we use it
To run the game and keep your account working, prevent duplicate names and cheating, fix crashes, and answer your requests. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party ads based on it.

## Who we share it with
We use service providers to run the game: Railway (server and database hosting) and Unity Technologies (engine diagnostics). Your player name may be visible to other players in the game. We may share information if the law requires it or to protect the game and its players.

## How long we keep it
We keep account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is hidden right away and permanently removed after 30 days. During those 30 days you can recover it with the recovery code the game shows you.

## Your choices and rights
- **Delete your account:** in the game, open Settings and choose "Delete account and data". Save the recovery code it shows. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
- **Recover a deleted account:** on the name screen, tap "Have a recovery code?" within 30 days.
- You may ask us for a copy of your information or ask us to correct it by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example under US state privacy laws or the GDPR), and we will respond as the law requires.

## Security
Data travels over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## International users
Our servers are located in the United States, so your information is processed there.

## Changes
If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept it.
